Chapter 1: The 25th of the month

Riya and Arjun Sharma live in Pune with their daughter Meera, who is 7. Together they bring home ₹85,000 a month. On the 1st, the account looks healthy. By the 25th, it's almost empty again, and nobody can say where it went.

So one evening, Riya opens the banking app and writes three things on a sheet of paper:

  • Came in: ₹85,000
  • 5 biggest spends: rent ₹22,000, groceries ₹11,500, food delivery and eating out ₹7,800, school fees ₹6,000, and the credit card's "minimum due" ₹4,000
  • Left on the last day: ₹1,900

"₹7,800 on food delivery?" Arjun reads it twice. Nobody is blaming anyone. For the first time, they can simply *see* it.
