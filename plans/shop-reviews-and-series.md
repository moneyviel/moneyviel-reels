# Shop plans (owner-approved 2026-11-20, build "later")

## A. Verified buyer ratings (build later)
- Only verified buyers can rate. After a captured Razorpay payment, the buyer gets a personal one-time "Rate your purchase" link signed with the payment ID + product slug (reuse Snippet 9 mv_pay_lookup / signing). Fake or other-product IDs fail.
- One rating per payment ID. Refunded payment = rating removed.
- Ask after 7 days (link on /my-downloads/ + one short "How's it going?" email), not at download.
- Questions: overall stars + "Easy to set up?", "Saved you time?", "Value for money?", optional comment (max 500 chars), first name + city (optional).
- 1-2 stars: show an instant help box ("Tell us what went wrong, we reply within 48 hours") - the rating still publishes; the buyer may update it later.
- Show average only after 5 ratings. "Verified buyer" badge. MoneyViel replies publicly.
- Optional with consent: Money Health Score journey (e.g. "62 -> 78 in 3 months"), never amounts.
- LEGAL: publish every genuine verified review, positive or negative (Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020 / CCPA guidance on fake reviews; IS 19000:2022). Remove only abusive, hateful, spam, off-topic or personal-data content, under a published Review Policy page. No incentives for positive reviews.
- Payhip: check its built-in buyer reviews and turn them on. Etsy has its own buyer-only reviews.

## B. Product marketing rhythm
- Mix: about 1 product post for every 4 education posts on each channel.
- Hero products (Money Command Centre, Emergency & Legacy Organiser, Kids Family Bundle): launch post + 3-4 follow-ups over ~10 days, each one feature told as a mini story, each linking to /shop/ and the YouTube demo.
- Smaller kits: one launch post, then a rotating "Kit of the week".
- Story-based mentions inside normal videos/posts (owner point 3) come later.
