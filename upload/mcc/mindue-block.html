Why the minimum due is a trap

Goes to interestActually reduces what you owe
  1. The minimum due is usually a small % of your balance. It is set so your account stays in good standing, not so you finish quickly.
  2. Interest is charged every month on whatever is left. When you pay only the minimum, a big part of it just covers that interest.
  3. As the balance shrinks, the minimum shrinks too, so progress gets slower and slower. That's the long tail you see in the chart.
  4. On many cards, carrying a balance can also mean new purchases start attracting interest straight away. Check your card's terms.
  5. The fix is simple: choose a fixed amount you can manage, pay it every month (even when the minimum drops), and pause new spending on the card if you can.

How we calculate: each month, interest = balance × (annual rate ÷ 12), new spending is added, then the payment is made. Minimum due = the bigger of the % and the floor. Real statements may differ because of fees, taxes and billing dates.
