Most families track money in three places at once: a notebook, a banking app and memory. The Money Command Centre puts it all in one Google Sheet that updates itself. You add what comes in and what goes out, and everything else is worked out for you.

What it shows you

  • A dashboard with money in, spent, saved and still in hand, plus 6 charts and monthly insights
  • A budget that fills itself from your spending, with amber and red alerts
  • Net worth, loans and EMIs, your emergency fund and your goals, all updating by themselves
  • Bills with 7-day and 30-day alerts, and a Money Health Score out of 100 with simple tips

Watch it work

In the video, a sample family adds one ₹1,200 spend for a birthday cake. Money spent on the dashboard moves from ₹85,055 to ₹86,255 straight away, and "Eating out" turns red because it has now gone over its monthly limit. That is the whole idea: one row in, a clear picture out.

How you get it

After payment you download a short access card. Click "Make my copy" and Google saves your own private copy in your Google Drive (a free Google account is all you need). There are two versions: one with a sample family's year filled in so you can explore, and one blank. It has no scripts and asks for no permissions. It is built for Google Sheets (not Excel) and shows amounts in rupees.
