How the race works

  • Every month, interest is added first (and 18% GST on it, if you switch that on).
  • 🔴 The Minimum Due car pays the higher of your minimum percentage or the minimum amount.
  • 🟡 The Fixed car pays your fixed amount (or the minimum due, if that is higher). 🟢 The Turbo car pays the fixed amount plus a little extra.
  • Pit stops are one-time extra payments to the car you are driving, like a bonus or a festival gift.
