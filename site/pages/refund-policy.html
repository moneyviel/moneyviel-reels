This Refund, Cancellation and Delivery Policy applies to digital products bought from the MoneyViel shop. It is part of our Terms and Conditions.

The short version

  • Our products are digital files that you can download straight after payment. Because files cannot be "returned", all sales are final once you have access to them.
  • We will always put things right if something goes wrong: if you were charged twice, did not receive your files, received broken files, or received the wrong product.
  • Please tell us within 7 days of your purchase.
  • Not sure if a product is right for you? Every product has a free sample you can download first.

When we will refund or fix your order

Please email us within 7 days of your purchase if:

  • You were charged twice for the same order. We will refund the extra payment.
  • You did not receive your files, for example the download page did not open after payment. We will send your files by email. If we cannot deliver them, we will give you a full refund.
  • A file is broken, for example it will not open or pages are missing. We will send you a working copy. If we cannot fix the problem, we will give you a full refund.
  • You received the wrong product. We will send the right one. If we cannot, we will give you a full refund.

When we cannot give a refund

Because you get the files straight away, we cannot give a refund if you simply change your mind, bought the product by mistake, or find that it is not what you expected after downloading it. Please read the product description and try the free sample before you buy.

Cancellations

Your order is complete as soon as your payment is successful, so it cannot be cancelled after that. If your payment failed but money was taken from your account, your bank or Razorpay will normally return it automatically within a few working days. If it does not come back, email us and we will help.

How to ask for a refund

Email support@moneyviel.com within 7 days of your purchase with:

  • your Razorpay payment ID (it starts with "pay_" and is shown in your payment receipt email);
  • the email address you used to pay;
  • what went wrong (a screenshot helps).

We will reply within 72 hours. If your refund is approved, we will send it to your original payment method through Razorpay. It usually reaches you within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.

Delivery

All our products are digital. Nothing is posted or shipped, and there are no delivery charges. You get your files on the download page straight after a successful payment; that page works for 24 hours, only in the browser and device used to buy. If you could not get them, use your payment ID once on our My downloads page (if it was not already used), or email support@moneyviel.com and we will send them.
