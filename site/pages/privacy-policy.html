This Privacy Policy explains what information MoneyViel ("we", "us") collects when you use www.moneyviel.com (the "Website"), why we collect it, who helps us process it, and the choices you have. MoneyViel is run from India. If you have any question about your privacy, email us at support@moneyviel.com.

The short version

  • We only collect what we need: for example your first name and email address if you join a newsletter, or the details you type into our contact form.
  • Our free money calculators and tools work inside your own browser. The numbers you type into them are not sent to us or stored by us.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • Our pages written for children (our Money for Kids stories and Kids Money Corner games) have no ads, no Google Analytics, no comments and no sign-up forms.

Information we collect

1. Newsletters. We run two free email newsletters: "MoneyViel Money Tips" (for adults aged 18 or older) and "Pip’s Money Club" (for parents and guardians of children aged 4–12). When you sign up we collect your first name and email address, and a record of your consent (the date and time and the technical details of your sign-up). Every sign-up must be confirmed by clicking a link in a confirmation email ("double opt-in"). We use this information only to send you the newsletter you chose. Our email provider may also record whether an email was opened or a link was clicked, so we can see which content is useful.

2. Contact form. If you use our contact form, we receive your name, email address and message so that we can reply to you.

3. Comments. If you leave a comment on one of our articles for adults, WordPress stores your name, email address, comment and IP address to display and moderate comments and to fight spam. Comments are switched off on our pages for children.

4. Likes and view counts. Our "like" button and page-view counter may use a cookie or your IP address so that the same visit or like is not counted twice.

5. Technical data. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) in server logs to keep the Website secure and working.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

  • Google Analytics helps us understand how our pages for adults are used (for example, which articles are popular). It is not used on our children’s pages.
  • Google AdSense may show ads on our pages for adults. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads, including ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Ads are never shown on our children’s pages. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by some third-party vendors at www.aboutads.info/choices (or www.youronlinechoices.eu in Europe).
  • Consent: visitors from the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent by Google’s certified consent message before advertising or analytics cookies are set. Until consent is given, Google Analytics may send limited cookieless signals that do not identify you. Visitors in certain US states can opt out of the sale or sharing of their information. You can change your choices at any time using the "Privacy & cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page.
  • Google reCAPTCHA protects our newsletter sign-up forms and our contact form from spam and bots. Google’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.
  • Translation: if you choose another language from our language menu, the page text is translated using Google Translate, and a small cookie remembers the language you chose.
  • Fonts and pictures: our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and author or commenter pictures may be loaded from Gravatar (Automattic). These services receive your IP address so they can deliver the files.
  • Videos: some pages include videos from our YouTube channels. On our pages for children these use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode, which does not store YouTube cookies before you press play; when a video plays, YouTube (Google) receives your IP address.
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the Website may not work properly without them.

Why we are allowed to use your information

  • Newsletters: your consent.
  • Contact form and comments: our legitimate interest in replying to you and running the discussion on our articles.
  • Analytics and advertising cookies: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, your consent through Google’s consent message; elsewhere, our legitimate interest in funding and improving the Website.
  • Server logs, spam protection and security: our legitimate interest in keeping the Website safe.
  • Records we must keep by law: legal obligation.
  • In India, we process personal data on the basis of your consent or for legitimate uses permitted by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who processes your information

We share information only with the service providers that help us run the Website, and only for the purposes described here:

  • Hostinger: website hosting and our email inbox.
  • EmailOctopus (United Kingdom): sends our newsletters and stores subscriber names and email addresses.
  • Google: Analytics, AdSense, reCAPTCHA, Translate, Fonts and YouTube, as described above.
  • Automattic: Gravatar pictures.
  • WordPress plugins that run on our own website: the contact form, the like button and the view counter.

Some of these providers store or process information outside India and outside your country. Where your information is transferred outside India, the EEA or the UK, we rely on our providers’ safeguards, such as adequacy decisions (for EmailOctopus in the UK), the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses (for Google). We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights or safety.

How long we keep information

  • Newsletter details: until you unsubscribe. After that we may keep only your email address on a "do not email" list so we never contact you again by mistake. Sign-ups that are never confirmed never receive our newsletter.
  • Contact messages: only as long as needed to reply and keep a reasonable record.
  • Comments: for as long as the comment is published, or until you ask us to remove it.
  • Server logs: for the limited period set by our hosting provider. Google Analytics data: no longer than 14 months.

Your choices and rights

  • Every newsletter email has an "unsubscribe" link. You can leave at any time.
  • You can ask to access, correct, update or delete your information, restrict or object to our use of it (including objecting to direct marketing at any time), receive a copy in a portable format, and withdraw your consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Email support@moneyviel.com; we will respond within 30 days.
  • If you are in India, you may also nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so.
  • Please raise any complaint with us first at support@moneyviel.com. If you are not satisfied, users in India may complain to the Data Protection Board of India, and users in the EU or UK to their local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office).

Children’s privacy

Some of our pages and stories (our Money for Kids stories and Kids Money Corner games) are written for children to enjoy together with a parent, guardian or teacher. On these pages we show no ads, use no Google Analytics, allow no comments and have no sign-up forms. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. Only adults can join our newsletters: "Pip’s Money Club" is for parents and guardians, and we never ask for a child’s name, age or any other details. Our /kids/ page (Pip’s Money Club) is written for parents: it contains the parent newsletter sign-up form, which uses Google reCAPTCHA. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please email support@moneyviel.com and we will delete it.

Links to other websites

Our pages may link to other websites, such as Amazon for our books. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no website or online service can be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Important changes will be announced on this page.
