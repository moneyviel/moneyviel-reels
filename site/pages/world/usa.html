Money basics in the USA

The American accounts, protections and free resources most families meet.

📌 Local things worth knowing

🏦 401(k) and IRAsA 401(k) is a workplace retirement plan; many employers add a match. An IRA is a retirement account you open yourself. Both have yearly contribution limits.Check current rules: IRS: retirement plans
🎯 Don't miss the employer matchIf your employer matches contributions, saving at least enough to get the full match is like a pay rise you would otherwise leave behind.Check current rules: IRS: 401(k) plans
🛡️ Bank deposits are insuredThe FDIC insures deposits at member banks up to a limit per depositor, per bank, per ownership category. Credit unions have similar cover through the NCUA.Check current rules: fdic.gov
📊 Free credit reportsYou can get your credit reports from the three bureaus for free at the official site. Check them for mistakes before big loans.Check current rules: annualcreditreport.com
💳 Struggling with card payments?If you cannot pay, call your card company early. Many can change your payment plan, and nonprofit credit counseling can help.Check current rules: consumerfinance.gov
🚨 Check before you investUse FINRA BrokerCheck to look up brokers and advisers before you hand over money.Check current rules: brokercheck.finra.org

✅ Your next three steps

  1. Build an emergency fund of three to six months of essential costs in an insured savings account.
  2. Pay more than the minimum on credit cards, starting with the highest interest rate.
  3. Save at least enough in your 401(k) to get the full employer match, if you have one.

🌍 What works the same everywhere

🛟 A safety cushion firstKeep a few months of essential costs in a safe, easy-to-reach account before you take any risks.
💳 Minimum payments keep you in debtPaying only the minimum on a credit card can stretch a small bill over many years. Our race shows how.
📈 Time beats timingSmall amounts invested regularly for many years usually matter more than picking the perfect moment.
🚩 If it sounds too good, walk awayGuaranteed high returns, pressure to decide today and "keep this secret" are classic signs of a scam.
