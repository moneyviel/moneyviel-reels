🛟 A safety cushion firstKeep a few months of essential costs in a safe, easy-to-reach account before you take any risks.
💳 Minimum payments keep you in debtPaying only the minimum on a credit card can stretch a small bill over many years. Our race shows how.
📈 Time beats timingSmall amounts invested regularly for many years usually matter more than picking the perfect moment.
