Money basics in the UK

The UK accounts, protections and free help that most households can use.

📌 Local things worth knowing

🧾 ISAs: tax-free savingIndividual Savings Accounts let you save or invest up to a yearly allowance with no tax on the interest or growth. The allowance does not roll over.Check current rules: GOV.UK: ISAs
🏦 Workplace pensionsMost employees are automatically enrolled in a workplace pension, with money added by you, your employer and tax relief. Opting out means losing your employer's share.Check current rules: GOV.UK: workplace pensions
🛡️ Savings protectionThe Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) protects deposits at authorised banks up to a limit per person, per banking licence.Check current rules: fscs.org.uk
🧓 State PensionYour State Pension depends on your National Insurance record. You can check your forecast and any gaps online.Check current rules: GOV.UK: State Pension forecast
🤝 Free, impartial helpMoneyHelper is a free, government-backed service for money and pension guidance, including free debt advice.Check current rules: moneyhelper.org.uk
🚨 Check before you investUse the FCA register to check that a firm is authorised, and its warning list to spot scams.Check current rules: FCA register

✅ Your next three steps

  1. Build an emergency fund in an easy-access savings account or cash ISA.
  2. Stay in your workplace pension and check you are getting the full employer contribution.
  3. If debts feel heavy, get free advice through MoneyHelper before borrowing more.

🌍 What works the same everywhere

🛟 A safety cushion firstKeep a few months of essential costs in a safe, easy-to-reach account before you take any risks.
💳 Minimum payments keep you in debtPaying only the minimum on a credit card can stretch a small bill over many years. Our race shows how.
📈 Time beats timingSmall amounts invested regularly for many years usually matter more than picking the perfect moment.
🚩 If it sounds too good, walk awayGuaranteed high returns, pressure to decide today and "keep this secret" are classic signs of a scam.
