Money basics in the UAE

For families living and working in the UAE, including the many expats who send money home.

📌 Local things worth knowing

💼 No personal income taxThe UAE does not tax individuals' salaries. VAT applies to most things you buy, and you may still owe tax in your home country, depending on its rules.Check current rules: u.ae
🎁 End-of-service gratuityPrivate-sector expats usually earn a gratuity after one year of continuous service, based on basic salary (not allowances). Some employers use the newer voluntary savings scheme instead.Check current rules: u.ae: end-of-service benefits
🧓 Plan your own retirementMost expats have no state pension in the UAE, so your gratuity and your own savings are your retirement. Start early, even with small amounts.Check current rules: MoHRE
📊 Your credit scoreBanks check your Al Etihad Credit Bureau (AECB) score before loans and cards. Late payments and bounced cheques can hurt it.Check current rules: aecb.gov.ae
🏠 Rent, school fees and bonusesBig yearly bills like rent cheques and school fees are easier when you save for them every month instead of borrowing.Check current rules: u.ae
🚨 Use licensed providers onlyBefore investing or sending money home, check that the bank, broker or exchange house is licensed by the UAE Central Bank or the right regulator.Check current rules: centralbank.ae

✅ Your next three steps

  1. Keep at least three to six months of costs in cash, more if your visa depends on your job.
  2. Know your basic salary and how much gratuity you have built up, and keep a copy of your contract.
  3. Pay credit cards in full each month: card interest in the UAE can be very high.

🌍 What works the same everywhere

🛟 A safety cushion firstKeep a few months of essential costs in a safe, easy-to-reach account before you take any risks.
💳 Minimum payments keep you in debtPaying only the minimum on a credit card can stretch a small bill over many years. Our race shows how.
📈 Time beats timingSmall amounts invested regularly for many years usually matter more than picking the perfect moment.
🚩 If it sounds too good, walk awayGuaranteed high returns, pressure to decide today and "keep this secret" are classic signs of a scam.
